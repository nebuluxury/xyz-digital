The details

What the search engines actually see

Not opinions, just what is in the code of your current site today.

  • One single page. Every menu item is a jump link on the same page. Google has one URL to rank, so it can only match your name.
  • Placeholder page description. Your meta description literally reads "Coastal Crest Notary Website" - the line Google shows in results is doing no selling.
  • Brand-only title tag. The title is just "Coastal Crest Notary" with no service or city, so you compete for nothing but your own name.
  • No structured data (schema). No local-business or service markup, so you are invisible to Google's rich results and the map pack.
  • No analytics. No Google Analytics or Search Console, so there is no way to know who visits, from where, or what they do.
  • No reviews or booking on-site. Nothing captures the visitor who wants to book at 9pm instead of calling.

Note: Google's PageSpeed score is included as a live screenshot in the call - the mobile number is the one that matters, and a hand-coded site wins it easily.
