Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 2026

Coastal Crest Notary is committed to making our website and our services accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. Accessibility is also central to the way we work: as a mobile notary, we come directly to homes, hospitals, care facilities, and other locations to serve clients who cannot easily travel.

Our website

We strive to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. Our site is built with semantic HTML, readable color contrast, keyboard-navigable menus, descriptive link text, and alternative text for meaningful images. We test on modern browsers and mobile devices.

Accessible, in-person service

  • We travel to you, so mobility is never a barrier to getting documents notarized.
  • We serve hospitals, hospice, assisted living, and homebound clients with patience and care.
  • We are happy to accommodate reasonable requests to make an appointment more comfortable - just let us know what you need.

Ongoing effort

Accessibility is an ongoing process and some content may not yet be fully optimized. We continue to improve the experience over time.
